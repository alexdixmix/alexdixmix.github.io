pc-admin

A private helper its owner uses to maintain his own Google Apps Script projects.

What pc-admin does

  • Shows how many runs of the owner's own Apps Script projects are going now and how many there were before, so that his tools stay within Google's limits.
  • Lists the versions and deployments of those projects.
  • When the owner asks for it, puts new code into one of his projects, makes a new version and switches its web app to that version, or switches it back to an earlier one.
  • Sends the owner an email about every change of code or version.

Who uses it

Only its owner. pc-admin works only with a short list of the owner's own projects, every request needs a secret key, and the app is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy

What pc-admin reads, what it keeps and what it never does is described in the Privacy Policy.
